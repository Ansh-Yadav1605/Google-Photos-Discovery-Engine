================================================================================
NEXTLEAP PRODUCT MANAGEMENT GRADUATION PROJECT — PART 1
PRODUCT: GOOGLE PHOTOS (CORE EXPERIENCE TEAM)
================================================================================

BUSINESS GOAL:
Increase the percentage of users who successfully retrieve a photo they remember
but cannot precisely describe when they start searching.

PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTION:
Why do users fail to retrieve old visual memories when they remember the photo
but cannot precisely describe it?

RESEARCH SCOPE & CORE CONSTRAINT:
This is NOT a generic "improve photo search" or "add conversational search" project.
The discovery engine specifically investigates retrieval friction where:
  1. The user knows a photo exists.
  2. The user remembers something about it (visual, temporal, spatial, emotional, or social cue).
  3. The user's memory is incomplete, imprecise, or distorted.
  4. The user attempts retrieval in Google Photos.
  5. Retrieval becomes difficult, frustrating, or completely unsuccessful.

CRITICAL RESEARCH PRINCIPLE:
Evidence-driven discovery. No predetermined solution. No assumption that AI,
semantic search, or conversational querying is the correct answer. The engine
must strictly separate evidence, user statements, observed behaviors, inferences,
hypotheses, and opportunity areas without fabricating quotes, reviews, or synthetic data.

DELIVERABLE FOR PART 1:
A real, functional, traceable AI-powered Discovery Engine that ingests multi-source
public user discussions, classifies relevance, extracts structured retrieval behavior,
clusters emergent problem patterns, compares transparent opportunity areas, and
synthesizes research findings as an empirical foundation for Parts 2–8.
